5S Methodology & Professional Portfolio Development

WEEK 1 | JULY 2026

Focused on establishing foundational organization by exploring the 5S principles for physical engineering workspaces, alongside developing a professional portfolio website to showcase my overall technical profile and document the 20-week program.

Topics Learned

The 5S framework encompassing Sort, Straighten, Shine, Standardize, and Sustain; methods to clean the workspace, check equipment, and establish clear rules for organization; structural design and UI/UX fundamentals for building a comprehensive professional portfolio website.

Key Takeaways

Removing unneeded items and giving every essential tool a clear place drastically reduces project friction; turning organizational rules into a daily routine is critical for sustained efficiency; structuring a physical workspace translates perfectly into structuring a clean, easily navigable digital portfolio.

Reflection

Organizing my physical and digital environments simultaneously set a strong baseline; building the website serves a highly effective dual purpose, acting as a live log for this 20-week program while establishing a polished, professional profile for my broader career in tech.
